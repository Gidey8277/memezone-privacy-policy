Privacy Policy for MemeZone

Effective Date: July 31, 2026

MemeZone values your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have when using the MemeZone mobile application.

Information We Collect

MemeZone may collect the following information:

  • Basic device information such as app version.
  • App usage information including features used, pages visited, downloads, and performance statistics.
  • Crash logs and diagnostic information to improve stability.
  • If you use the AI Meme Generator, the text prompts you submit may be processed to generate memes.
  • If you upload your own images for meme creation, those images are processed only to generate your requested meme.
  • If you contact us, we may collect your email address and any information you include in your message.

How We Use Your Information

We use collected information to:

  • Provide meme browsing and downloading features.
  • Create memes using the manual or AI Meme Generator.
  • Improve app performance and reliability.
  • Detect and fix bugs.
  • Prevent abuse and misuse of the service.
  • Respond to customer support requests.

AI Meme Generator

When you use AI-powered meme generation, your prompts and, if applicable, uploaded images may be securely transmitted to our AI service providers to generate the requested content. These inputs are used only for processing your request.

Downloaded Content

MemeZone allows users to download memes to their devices. Downloaded files are stored locally on your device. We do not access or manage files after they have been saved to your device.

Advertising

MemeZone displays advertisements through trusted advertising partners such as Google AdMob. These partners may collect anonymous identifiers, device information, and advertising data to provide and improve advertisements. You can manage ad personalization through your Google account or your device settings.

Third-Party Services

MemeZone may use trusted third-party services for analytics, advertising, cloud storage, and AI content generation. These providers process data according to their own privacy policies.

Data Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures to protect your information. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children's Privacy

MemeZone is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we become aware that such information has been collected, we will delete it promptly.

Your Choices

You may:

  • Stop using the app at any time.
  • Delete downloaded memes from your device.
  • Limit personalized advertising through your device settings.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated Effective Date.
